How 14 days actually works

The speed comes from the planning. Here is the build, day by day.

Before Day 1 Design, permits and every material on site.

We finalize the layout, finishes and fixtures with you, pull permits, and stage everything at your home. The clock starts only when nothing is left to wait on.

  1. Day 1

    Protect & demo

    Floors and hallways covered, the old bathroom removed and hauled away.

  2. Days 2–3

    Rough plumbing & electrical

    Drains, valves, lighting and heated-floor wiring moved to the new layout.

  3. Day 4

    Inspection & waterproofing

    Rough inspection, then a full waterproofing system on every wet wall and floor.

  4. Days 5–8

    Tile & stone

    Large-format floors, hand-set zellige, niches and benches, laid and grouted.

  5. Days 9–10

    Vanity & stone tops

    Custom vanity installed, stone tops templated and set.

  6. Days 11–12

    Glass, fixtures & lighting

    Shower glass, brass fixtures, mirrors, sconces and hardware.

  7. Day 13

    Final inspection & detail

    Sign-off, caulk lines, touch-ups and a deep clean.

  8. Day 14

    Walkthrough & handover

    We walk the room with you, and it is yours.
