Six Meta and Instagram concepts for the 14-day luxury bathroom offer. Each comes as a 1:1 feed post and a 9:16 Story/Reel cover, with copy ready to paste into Ads Manager. Every ad sends people to the landing page, where the only action is booking a design call.

Launch plan for this weekend

Audience
  • Location: Kuarto's service ZIP codes in Southern California (start with the highest-value areas)
  • Age 30–65, Advantage+ audience on, with interest suggestions: interior design, home improvement, luxury goods, Architectural Digest, Houzz
  • Exclude existing customers once a list is uploaded
Structure & budget
  • One campaign, one ad set, all six concepts as separate ads so Meta finds the winner
  • Start around $50–100 per day for the first 5–7 days
  • Judge on cost per booked call, not clicks; cut any ad with 3× target spend and no lead
Before you hit publish
  • Add the real Calendly link and Meta Pixel to the landing page
  • Fill in phone, starting price and slot count
  • Get the contractor's sign-off on the 14-day promise wording and any price shown
  • Keep copy about the bathroom, not the person (Meta rejects "Are you a homeowner?" style lines)
