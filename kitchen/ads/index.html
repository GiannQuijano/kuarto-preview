Reel scripts

Shoot on a phone at a finished job or mid-build. Real footage of the builder usually beats polished video for booked calls.

"Your kitchen in 12 weeks"

~25 SEC · BUILDER TO CAMERA

0–3sBuilder in a finished kitchen, hand on the island stone."Most kitchen remodels take nine months. We do them in twelve weeks."
3–10sFast cuts: cabinet shop, demo, plumbing rough-in."We build your cabinets in our shop while we demo and rough-in at your house. Same team the whole way."
10–18sSlow pan: slab backsplash, range, integrated fridge."Custom cabinetry, custom stone, and right now the fridge and range are on us."
18–25sBuilder back to camera. Text: "Free design call"."If you're in Southern California, book a call. Link's right there."

~20 SEC · TIMELAPSE, NO VOICE

Weekend test plan

Enough spend to see which hook books calls, without committing a big budget before the offer is proven.

Campaign setup

  • Objective: Leads, optimized for the Lead event fired when the form on the landing page is submitted.
  • Budget: $100/day for 3 days, one ad set, Advantage+ placements.
  • Ads live: 01 to 04 plus Stories 01 and 02. Let Meta spread spend; don't split budgets by hand yet.
  • Retargeting: Ad 05 at $20/day once the site has 200+ visitors.

Audience

  • Location: 25–40 mi radius around the service area, or a list of higher-value ZIPs [confirm with contractor].
  • Age: 30–65.
  • Suggestions: interior design, home renovation, kitchen remodeling, Houzz, Architectural Digest, Dwell.
  • Check whether Meta flags the campaign as a Special Ad Category for your account. If so, targeting narrows to location only.

What to watch

  • Monday read: cost per form lead and cost per booked call, by ad.
  • Keep any ad booking calls under $150 each. Pause ads with $150 spent and no leads.
  • Quality: track how many leads pick $125k+ on the budget question. If most pick "Not sure yet," move Ad 03 up.
  • Speed to lead: call every form fill within 15 minutes, even before they book.
Before launch: host the landing page on Kuarto's domain (or a simple host like Netlify or Framer), paste in the real Calendly link, install the Meta Pixel, and fill in the license number, phone and offer end date. The photos are AI design concepts, not past Kuarto jobs, so don't caption them as client projects. Replace them with real project photos once you have them and keep the type and layout. Confirm the "limited build slots" and appliance value claims with the contractor before running.
