How the 12 weeks run

A schedule you can plan your life around.

Cabinetry is built in the shop while demo and rough-in happen at your home. Running those in parallel is how we finish in about three months.

WEEKS 1–2

Design

Measure, layout, elevations, 3D walkthrough. Slab and appliance selection.

WEEKS 3–5

Demo & rough-in

Old kitchen out. Plumbing, gas and electrical moved. Inspections passed. Cabinets in the shop.

WEEKS 6–8

Cabinetry

Drywall, then custom cabinetry installed and leveled. Stone templated off the real boxes.

WEEKS 9–10

Stone

Counters, waterfall island and backsplash fabricated and set.

WEEKS 11–12

Finish

Appliances, fixtures, lighting, paint, punch list and final walkthrough.

Questions

Before you book

How can a full kitchen really take 12 weeks?

We build your cabinets in the shop while demo and rough-in happen at your home, and we schedule every trade ourselves. Nothing waits on a handoff between companies. The schedule is set before demo and you see it week by week.

Is the refrigerator and range really free?

Yes. Every full kitchen rebuild booked during this offer includes a top-of-the-line refrigerator and range at no cost. We select the models with you during design so they fit the layout and the cabinetry is built around them.

What does a Kuarto kitchen cost?

Most full rebuilds land in the low-to-mid six figures depending on size, layout changes and materials. You'll get a realistic range on the design call and a fixed proposal after the in-home measure.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. We seal off the work area, keep the rest of the house clean, and can set up a temporary kitchen station if you want one.

Where do you work?

Across Southern California. Share your ZIP code when you book and we'll confirm.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Kuarto Construction is a licensed California general contractor and fully insured. We pull every permit for your project.
